About Snehal Creations

Hi! I'm Snehal, an artist based in Pune with a deep love for handmade crafts. Through Snehal Creations, I pour my heart into every piece — be it intricate embroidery, sparkling resin art, expressive paintings, or one-of-a-kind gifts.

My goal is simple: to turn everyday moments into art. Whether you're looking for a unique home décor piece, a heartfelt gift for a loved one, or something completely custom — I'm here to make it for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom order take? ▼
Delivery time varies by product. Typically 5–15 business days depending on complexity. Wedding/bulk orders may need more time — please reach out early!
Do you ship outside Pune? ▼
Yes! I ship all across India. Shipping charges will be shared at the time of order confirmation.
Can I see a preview before the final piece? ▼
Absolutely! For most custom orders, I share a work-in-progress photo for your approval before completing the piece.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
I accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and cash on delivery for local Pune orders. 50% advance is required for custom orders.
Can I order a gift with a personalised message? ▼
Yes! Just mention it in your order details. I'll include a handwritten note and can also do gift wrapping on request. 🎁
